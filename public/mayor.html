City leadership

Mayor Matt Santini

Official public biography and office contact details from the City of Cartersville.

Mayor since 2008

A public-service profile

Matt Santini began serving as Cartersville's mayor in 2008. The City says he works with City Council and residents to preserve the community's quality of life while guiding a growing city.

Santini earned a Bachelor of Science from Georgia Southern University in Marketing/Sales, Sales Management, and Advertising. His professional experience includes managing a local radio station and administering a local nonprofit organization.

The City profile says Santini was born in Fairport, New York, grew up in Marietta, and has lived in Cartersville since 1991. It lists golf, football, baseball, and reading among his interests.

His published community service includes board service with the Cartersville Downtown Development Authority and The Autumn Leaves Project, leadership and board roles with the Georgia Association of Broadcasters, and membership in the Rotary Club of Etowah.

  • Cartersville Downtown Development Authority board member, 2003–2007
  • Downtown Development Authority president, 2006–August 2007
  • Rotary Club of Etowah charter member, 2002; club president, 2003
  • The Autumn Leaves Project board member, 2016–present (per city profile)
  • Georgia Association of Broadcasters board member, 2003–2017
  • Georgia Association of Broadcasters president, 2012
  • Georgia Association of Broadcasters Broadcaster of the Year, 2013
